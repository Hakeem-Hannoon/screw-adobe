## Description (4000 max)

```
Black out anything in a PDF, for real. Then sign it, merge it, split it, shrink it or turn it into Word. HackerPDF is a complete PDF toolkit that runs entirely on your iPhone or iPad.

Free. No ads. No account. No subscriptions. Your files are never uploaded: every tool works on your device, even in Airplane Mode.

REDACTION THAT ACTUALLY REMOVES TEXT
Drawing a black box over a PDF usually hides nothing: the words are still underneath and anyone can copy them out. HackerPDF rebuilds every page you edit with your boxes burned in, so the original text is gone from the file. The saved copy is a brand-new PDF, without the original's title, author, hidden metadata, bookmarks, attachments or scripts.
• Drag to cover anything with a black or white box
• Type over a white-out to replace a value
• Find and cover every match of a name or number at once
• One tap to cover every email address, phone number or long number (card, account, SIN or SSN)
• Delete pages you don't want to share
• Flatten every page for the strongest option

SIGN
Type your name and pick from 20 handwriting styles, or draw your signature with your finger. Choose black, ballpoint blue or dark blue ink, tap where it goes, and drag or resize it. Sign many pages at once.

EVERY OTHER PDF TOOL
• Merge PDFs and drag them into order
• Split a PDF anywhere, or every few pages
• Organize: reorder, delete or extract pages
• Rotate pages
• Compress PDFs to a target size, keeping text selectable
• Add page numbers
• Compare two versions of a PDF, word by word
• Make scanned PDFs searchable with on-device OCR (English)

CONVERT
• PDF to Word, with real headings, lists and tables
• Word to PDF, with selectable text
• PDF to images (PNG or JPG) and images to PDF
• PDF to text or Markdown

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
HackerPDF collects no data. There is no sign-in, no analytics, no tracking and no internet connection required. Finished files are saved to Files › On My iPhone › HackerPDF, and the share sheet opens so you can send them anywhere you like. Photos turned into PDFs lose their hidden location data too.

Password-protected PDFs open after you enter the password, and the password never leaves your device.
```
