# Age Rating

[[Home]] · [[App Store/App Store Connect Answers|Answers]]

App Store Connect › App Information › Age Ratings › **Set Up Age Ratings** (or **Edit**). Apple calculates the rating from these answers; with all of them as below, HackerPDF is rated **4+** everywhere. This covers Apple's 2025 questionnaire (in-app controls, capabilities, medical/wellness, violent themes) and the social-media questions that became required in September 2026.

## In-app controls

| Item | Answer | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Parental Controls | **Not selected** | None in the app. |
| Age Assurance | **Not selected** | No age checks; nothing needs them. |

## Capabilities

| Item | Answer | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unrestricted Web Access | **No** | There's no browser. A link inside a converted document opens in Safari, outside the app. |
| User-Generated Content | **No** | Users edit their own files; nothing is distributed to other users. |
| Social Media | **No** | No feeds, profiles or sharing between users. |
| Messaging and Chat | **No** | — |
| Advertising | **No** | No ads of any kind. |

**Expected result: 4+.** If the summary shows anything higher, one answer above was mis-clicked.

The questionnaire's wording occasionally changes. If you meet a question not listed here, the honest answer for HackerPDF is almost always "None" / "No": it's an offline document tool with no content of its own.
