Privacy policy

HackerPDF (website and iPhone/iPad app) · Effective November 4, 2026

HackerPDF collects no data about you. Your files are opened, edited and saved on your own device and are never uploaded anywhere.

Your files

Every tool (redact, sign, merge, split, organize, rotate, compress, compare, page numbers, OCR and every conversion) runs entirely on your device. Files you open are read into the app's memory, processed there, and written back to your device when you save. No copy is sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see your files, and we keep no record that you used them.

The website enforces this with a Content-Security-Policy that blocks the page from sending data to any other server. The iPhone and iPad app contains everything it needs and makes no network requests at all; it works in Airplane Mode.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no tracking, no cookies and no third-party software development kits that collect data. We do not sell, share or rent any information, because we have none.

Where saved files go in the app

When you save a finished file in the iPhone or iPad app, it is stored in the app's own folder on your device, which you can open in the Files app under On My iPhone (or On My iPad) › HackerPDF. The app then opens Apple's share sheet so you can save the file somewhere else or send it. Anything you send from the share sheet goes wherever you choose (for example Mail, Messages or iCloud Drive) and is then covered by that service's own policy. If you back up your device with iCloud or a computer, files in the app's folder may be included in that backup, under Apple's terms.

To delete saved files, delete them in the Files app, or delete the app.

Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photo library. When you choose files or images, iOS shows its own picker and gives the app only the items you pick.

Children

HackerPDF does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

The website host

The website is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses when the page is loaded; see GitHub's privacy statement. Your files are never sent to GitHub. The app does not use GitHub or any other server.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. A change that collects any data would be stated plainly here before it takes effect.
