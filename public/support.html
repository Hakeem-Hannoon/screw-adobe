Where do my saved files go?

On iPhone and iPad, open the Files app and go to On My iPhone (or On My iPad) › HackerPDF. When you save, the share sheet also opens so you can choose Save to Files, Mail, AirDrop and so on. On the website, files go to your browser's downloads.

Is redaction really permanent?

Yes. Every page you edit is rebuilt as an image with your boxes burned in, so the text underneath is gone from the file, not just hidden. Pages you didn't touch keep their selectable text. For the strongest option, open the arrow next to Download and turn on Flatten every page.

Do I need an account or internet?

No account, ever. The app works fully offline. It is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

A password-protected PDF won't open

Enter its password when asked. It stays on your device. The saved copy has no password.

A scanned PDF has no text

Scans are pictures of letters. Use Make searchable to read them with on-device OCR (English) first; then Find, PDF to text and PDF to Word can use the text.

Word to PDF shows the wrong font

Calibri, Cambria, Arial, Times New Roman and Courier New are matched exactly; other fonts are swapped for the closest of these. Old .doc files need saving as .docx first.
